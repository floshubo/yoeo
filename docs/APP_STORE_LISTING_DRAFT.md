## Description

**YOEO — Eat Out, Not In the Dark**

Eating out with food allergies means constant vigilance: reading every menu line by line, asking staff, second-guessing every dish. YOEO helps reduce the guesswork.

**HOW IT WORKS**

Point your camera at a menu and allergen list, printed, handwritten, or shared as a photo. YOEO reads the text and cross-references it against your personal allergen profile.

**BUILT ON EU AND US STANDARDS**

YOEO includes profile categories covering the 14 EU allergen groups and the nine major US food allergens, so you can keep your preferences in one place when traveling.

**YOUR PROFILE, YOUR RULES**

Set up your profile once. YOEO remembers your allergen preferences so every scan can be personalized.

**WHAT YOEO IS — AND ISN'T**

YOEO is a reading and flagging tool. It helps you catch what a menu says. It does not replace asking staff about cross-contact or verifying ingredients for severe allergies. Always confirm with restaurant staff when the stakes are high.

For people who don't want food anxiety to be the main course.
