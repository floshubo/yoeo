YOEO Support

Help with menu scans, accounts and subscriptions.

Scanning

Use a sharp, well-lit photo with the full menu text inside the frame. If the menu has a separate allergen legend, photograph that first and then add the menu pages. Always confirm ingredients and cross-contact with restaurant staff.

Account and data

Your profile stays on this device. Use the account screen to sign in or out, and App Settings to export or delete your data. If you are signed in, deleting data also permanently deletes your account and its associated database data.

Subscriptions

Open Profile → Subscription to purchase, restore or manage YOEO Pro. App Store and Google Play subscriptions can also be managed in the device’s store account settings.

Report a problem

Email YOEO.health@gmail.com. If you received YOEO through TestFlight, you can also use the TestFlight app’s “Send Beta Feedback” action. Include what you tapped, what you expected, and your device model.
